About the bakery

Baked with purpose

Kelvin Loaf Bakery is an income-generating project for Kenya Children's Homes (KCH) that produces bread and a variety of other baked products.

The bakery supplies bread to KCH programs and also makes products such as scones, cookies, cakes, and mandazis to help reduce costs and support sustainability.
